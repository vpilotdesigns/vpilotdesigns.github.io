🚨 **Update Released!** ✈️  
📍 **SKBO – El Dorado International Airport v1.1** by V Pilot Designs  

We remain committed to maintaining realism, and we’re excited to present the next update for **El Dorado International Airport** in **MSFS 2024** with some highly requested additions and refinements.  

### What’s New in v1.1  
✨ Classic **“EL DORADO” sign** added to the old terminal buildings in commemoration of the airport’s 65th anniversary  
🛫 New **remote stands 62–68** added between Terminal 1 and Terminal 2  
🔧 Miscellaneous **fixes and adjustments** for improved accuracy and performance  

✈️ Thanks for flying with **V Pilot Designs** and for supporting Colombian airports in MSFS!
