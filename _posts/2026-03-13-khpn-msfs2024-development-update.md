## Development Update – KHPN for MSFS 2024 ✈️

We’ve been working hard behind the scenes bringing **KHPN – Westchester County Airport** to **MSFS 2024**, focusing on adding the level of detail and precision that our airports are known for.

This development has faced a couple of delays and obstacles along the way, but over the past few months we’ve been fully focused on the project and will continue pushing forward until release.

Every asset, surface, and structure is being carefully refined to ensure the final product delivers the quality and realism the community expects. We’re getting closer every day to a finished build before it moves into **beta testing**.

There are still a few weeks of development ahead, but the extra time allows us to continue pushing the **quality and level of detail** we’re adding to the airport.

For those who already purchased **KHPN for MSFS 2020**, there will be **a small upgrade fee (~$5)** to move to the **MSFS 2024** version.
