🚨 **NOW AVAILABLE for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024!** ✈️  
📍 **SKAR – El Edén International Airport (Armenia, Colombia)** by V Pilot Designs

We’re excited to announce the release of **SKAR El Edén International Airport**, now available for **Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024**!  
Located in the heart of Colombia’s coffee region, this airport serves as a key gateway to Armenia and the Quindío region.

> This version has been carefully enhanced for MSFS 2024 — rebuilt to take full advantage of the sim’s new lighting, material, and terrain capabilities.  
> **Available as a FREE upgrade** to all owners of the MSFS 2020 version!

### ✈️ Key Features
- Built natively for **MSFS 2024** with updated SDK standards  
- **Custom terminal**, control tower, and buildings  
- **Accurate taxiway network**, parking stands, and runway layout  
- **Full PBR textures** for realistic materials and reflections  
- Optimized models for **smooth performance**  including LODs
- Realistic **night lighting** and terrain integration

Whether you’re flying short domestic hops or scenic regional routes through Colombia’s coffee mountains, SKAR delivers the perfect mix of performance and realism.

📦 **Now available on the Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 Marketplace.**
